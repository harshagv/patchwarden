The Patchwarden plugin

  1. Download the plugin.
  2. In WordPress: Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, choose patchwarden.zip, Install, Activate.
  3. Press "Get your plugin key" below and copy it.
  4. In WordPress: Settings > Patchwarden, paste the key, press "Save and connect". Done.

The plugin never asks for a password. Deleting it in WordPress removes its settings and switches its key off.
